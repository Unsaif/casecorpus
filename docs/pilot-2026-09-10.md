# Pilot run — 10 September 2026

Corpus counts (PubMed, MeSH exploded): 41,933 IEM case reports; 5,298 with PMC full text; 2,669 in the
PMC open-access subset; 15,107 since 2010 (3,784 in PMC). All inborn genetic disease: 180,007.

Pilot slice: 20 most recent PubMed records matching the seed query with the OA filter, 2023–2026.
Only 8 of the 20 were already in Europe PMC (lag of weeks for new PMC articles), so full text was
taken from PMC efetch (db=pmc) instead — now tier 1a in the design. 12 full texts were fetched;
11 were extracted (one run was cut off by a usage limit and can be re-run with
`casecorpus extract` or another Cowork session — PMID 42607148).

Extractor: Claude (Cowork subagents), prompt version 0.1.0, one triage pass + one record per
affected individual; every evidence quote checked to occur verbatim in the rendered source.

Results (export/): 18 individuals, 422 phenotypes, 482 measurements, 6 enzyme activities, 31 variants,
52 diagnoses, 68 treatments; 18/18 records schema-valid and phenopacket-valid.
Grounding, automatic: HPO 252 grounded / 168 candidate / 2 unresolved; analytes 161 grounded /
115 candidate / 206 unresolved (mostly general lab analytes outside the IEM lexicon — weight,
PT/INR, total protein, eGFR); genes 31/31; diagnoses 29 grounded / 23 candidate.
FINAL diagnosis tiers: 17 MOLECULAR, 3 BIOCHEMICAL, 1 CLINICAL.

Observations worth keeping:
- Case series and family papers put per-patient data in tables; the r/c anchored grid rendering
  worked (the MADD family paper yielded 39 + 29 + 23 acylcarnitine measurements from one table).
- Extractors surfaced text-vs-table conflicts in 5 of 11 papers (e.g. 3-methylglutaric vs
  3-methylglutaconic acid; LDL "low" in text, normal in table). Recording both plus a note is the
  right behaviour; a curator view of these notes is needed.
- Authors' interpretation and stated reference ranges disagree in a handful of measurements; the
  validator flags them (kept as warnings, not errors).
- Ages are often narrative ("in his early 30s", "middle childhood"); the no-estimation rule leaves
  iso8601 null and keeps the text, as intended.
- The per-record confidence heuristic is dominated by unit warnings and is not yet meaningful.
